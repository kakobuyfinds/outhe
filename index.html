Sizing and QC photo checklist

Shoes

Compare insole length in centimeters with a pair that fits. Ask for photos of both size labels, both outsoles and the heels side by side.

Hoodies, T-shirts and jackets

Compare flat chest width, body length and sleeve length. For a jacket you plan to layer, measure one that fits over a sweatshirt. Request close-ups of the print, lining or zipper where relevant.

Pants and shorts

Compare waistband width, rise and inseam. If the waist is elastic, ask for both relaxed and stretched measurements.

Estimate your shipping budget

Add the item price, domestic shipping, service and payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Convert the total using the exchange rate your payment method applies.

Compare shipping quotes for your destination using the packed parcel weight and dimensions. Bulky packaging can raise the charge on routes that use volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping directory that groups product listings by category so you can compare options and save the items you want to review.

Are the catalog images warehouse QC photos?

The images here come from product listings. If warehouse QC is available, use photos of your selected item to verify its size, color and condition.

Where do I check current prices and purchase options?

Open the item on HipobuyFind and check the seller listing for your selected size or color. The destination listing provides the current purchase options.
